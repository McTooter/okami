## Direction for Okami

The redesign will use an original **indexed gallery** treatment: warm paper-white artwork fields, deep graphite app chrome, slim acid-lime playback signals, and an off-axis editorial grid. The tab bar will become a compact floating command dock with strong active-state geometry rather than an ordinary five-icon strip. The Library will use a featured album module, a concise collection rail, and sharp track rows with intentional rhythm and tactile press feedback.
